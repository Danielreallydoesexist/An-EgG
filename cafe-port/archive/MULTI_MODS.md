# Multiple creations in one mod

Each mod supports up to three bosses, three biomes, and six custom spawnables.
Open one of those collections from the mod home screen. Its cards show every
creation in that category; tap one to edit it or tap **+ ADD** to make another.

- **BOSSES:** Each boss has its own name, color, moves, art, hitbox, difficulty, and music.
- **BIOMES:** Each biome has its own name, gradient, and object choices. The first appears right of the starting desk, the second below it, and the third left of it. They can also appear farther out.
- **SPAWNABLES:** Each has its own name, behavior, art, and hitbox. Tap **SUMMON** to choose which boss that element starts, or choose **NOTHING**.
- **CHOOSE OBJECTS:** Use **PAGE 1 / 2** to see **CUSTOM 4–6**. Enable a custom type in each biome where it should spawn.

While editing one creation, use **ALL** to return to its collection,
**COPY THIS** to duplicate its settings and painted art, **RENAME** to name it,
or **DELETE** to remove it after confirmation. At least one of each type must
remain. If you delete a boss, spawnables bound to it stop summoning until you
choose a new boss. Press **X** to save from any editor screen.

Ordinary buttons always start UNDEF. A custom spawnable starts only its selected boss. Existing single-boss mods still load.

In a boss's **FIGHT SETTINGS**, tap **I**, **II**, and **III** to choose its
phases. At least one remains on. The fight starts on the first selected phase,
then moves through the others in order. Phase III begins at half health.
Ordinary UNDEF always plays all three phases.
